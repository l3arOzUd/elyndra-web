Reference images only. File existence and alpha checks do not prove visual consistency or animation readiness.

A round four-legged seed with a mossy shell and two twig antennae; friendly face; earth-green palette.

Sporeling mob_0002

A waddling mushroom with a speckled cream-and-rust cap, stubby root feet, puffs of glowing spores.

Ambermite mob_0003

A beetle-sized six-legged mite under a translucent amber shell with a glowing core; honey-orange.

Lunari mob_0004

A sugar-glider-like beast with leaf-shaped ears and a banded tail, membrane glide flaps; moonlit silver-green.

Craglet mob_0005

A squat lizard covered in slate plates, quartz flecks along the spine, a gear glinting in its belly seam.

Thornback mob_0006

A stocky boar with a back of woody thorns and bark-like hide; mossy tusks.

Dustwing mob_0007

A large moth with sandy, powder-dusted wings and brass-coloured eyespots; trailing dust motes.

Brookfin mob_0008

A fat fish on stubby fin-legs, glass-clear fins showing faint aether veins; blue-green.

Reedstalker mob_0009

A lanky stalk-creature woven from reeds, a seed-pod head that opens to spit darts; marsh green and brown.

Rotroot mob_0010

A knot of rotting roots with a glowing sap core, crawling on root-legs; dark wood, sickly teal glow.

Barkwarden mob_0011

A man-high wooden guardian with copper-jointed limbs, a cracked lens eye and moss in the seams.

Graftling mob_0012

A knee-high bundle of living twigs on three folding brass hinge-legs, a jade sap bead glowing at the joint; leaf tufts for a head; bark brown, brass, jade.

Sapdrone mob_0013

A fist-sized brass beetle-drone with four glassy wings and a long needle proboscis, its belly tank sloshing with glowing jade sap.

Vinecrawler mob_0014

A spider-shaped brass gear-frame wrapped and pulled by thick green vines, leaves sprouting from the gear teeth, a cracked jade lens at the core.

Root Sentinel mob_0015

A three-metre humanoid guard of brass and stone, completely laced with thick roots, a lantern-like jade eye under a hood of bark.

Sandcoil mob_0016

A thick, short serpent coiled like a spring, scales the colour and grain of saffron sand, ember-orange belly, heat shimmer above it.

Glass Scarab mob_0017

A dog-sized scarab whose shell is clear amber glass with a glowing ember core showing through, legs of dark chitin.

Dune Sentry mob_0018

A blocky humanoid of carved sandstone with brass bands at the joints, a slit visor glowing orange, sand pouring from its cracks.

Sun Warden mob_0019

A tall bronze guardian with a halo-ring of sun mirrors on its back, a faceless polished face, a long spear-gnomon.
